§ 01 — Why most agentic AI stalls

The demo works. Then what?

The gap between a slick proof-of-concept and a system your team relies on is where most budgets disappear. That gap is exactly what I close.

It's unreliable

Agents that hallucinate, loop, or break on edge cases the demo never hit. No evals, no guardrails, no observability.

It doesn't integrate

A clever notebook that never touches your real tools, data, or workflows — so it never creates value.

It's not safe to trust

No controls around data, cost, or failure modes. Fine for a toy; unacceptable for anything that matters.

Nobody owns it

Built by a vendor who leaves, or a team without agentic depth. It rots the moment it's handed over.
