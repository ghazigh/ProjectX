# Buyer-directory kit

Places where buyers *actively search* for someone to build agentic AI — so you
get found and contacted without posting "hire me" to your network. Being listed
in a directory is passive and normal; it doesn't read as soliciting.

> **Conflict-of-interest note:** a public "available for hire" listing is still
> public. Check your employment contract before listing, and keep wording about
> your current employer minimal. Directories are lower-profile than posting to
> your own feed, but they're not invisible.

## Ranked for your profile

| Priority | Platform | Why | Effort |
|----------|----------|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Toptal** | Premium, vetted network; clients come *to it* to hire senior AI talent at real rates. Your PhD + IEEE record clears the bar most can't. | Apply (screening) |
| 2 | **Clutch / The Manifest** | B2B buyers browse here for AI/ML consultants and read reviews. Free listing; one good review compounds. | Free profile |
| 3 | **GitHub (as a portfolio)** | CTOs and founders vet builders by their code. A clean profile + 1-2 polished agentic repos = passive technical credibility that gets you DMed. | Ongoing |
| 4 | **Wellfound (AngelList)** | Startups with budget searching for AI builders/advisors. Passive inbound from funded teams. | Free profile |
| 5 | **Contra** | No-fee freelance marketplace, decent for premium AI work; buyers search it. | Free profile |
| 6 | **Google Business Profile** | Captures "AI consultant near me / agentic AI development" searches. Free, and surfaces you in Maps/Search. | Free |
| 7 | **Niche AI directories** | "AI consultant" / "AI agency" directories and curated lists (search "agentic AI development companies" to find where competitors are listed, then get listed there too). | Varies |

Skip generic low-end gig sites — they attract price-shoppers and waste your
credentials.

## Reusable profile copy (adapt per platform)

**Headline / title:**
> Agentic AI Engineer & Researcher — production-grade AI agents

**Short bio (≈50 words):**
> I design and build agentic AI systems that survive production — not just the
> demo. PhD AI researcher (ÉTS Montréal), IEEE-published, with agentic AI
> experience in financial services. I bring research-grade rigor and
> finance-grade reliability to AI agents: evaluation, guardrails, observability,
> and clean integration. Available for select audits, builds, and fractional
> engagements.

**Services to list:** Agentic AI Audit & Roadmap · Agentic AI Build Sprint ·
Fractional Agentic AI Lead. (See `sales/agentic-ai-offer.md`.)

**Always link** the listing to your service page (`work-with-me.html`) — the
directory gets you found; the page does the convincing.

## The habit

Listing once does little. Quarterly: refresh profiles, add any new proof (a
shipped project, a new article, a testimonial). Reviews and recency are what move
you up directory rankings.
