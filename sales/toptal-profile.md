## Tips
- Lead every answer with **reliability and evidence**, not model/framework
  name-drops — that's your differentiator vs. the flood of "I do AI" applicants.
- Have the `reliable-agent` repo public and polished before the technical
  interview; offer to screen-share a walkthrough.
- For the test project, treat it as a paid client would expect: tests, a clear
  README, and a note on how you'd take it to production.
- Set your rate at senior level. Underpricing signals junior on Toptal.
