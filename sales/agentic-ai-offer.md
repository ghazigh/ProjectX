# Productized offer — agentic AI consulting & build

A clear ladder so a buyer knows exactly what they're getting. Positioned around
your edge: **research-grade rigor + finance-grade reliability** → "agentic AI
that survives production."

## The ladder

| # | Offer | Price (starting) | Length | Purpose |
|---|-------|------------------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Agentic AI Audit & Roadmap** | from $3,500 | 1–2 wks | Low-risk entry. Diagnose ROI + deliver a build plan. |
| 2 | **Agentic AI Build Sprint** | from $12,000 | 4–6 wks | The core $10k+ project. One agent/workflow, production-grade. |
| 3 | **Fractional Agentic AI Lead** | from $4,000/mo | monthly | Recurring revenue. Ongoing direction + reliability. |

Natural path: Audit → Build → Fractional. The audit lowers the buyer's risk and
gets you in the door; the build is the $10k cheque; the retainer compounds.

## What makes the price defensible

You're not selling "I'll build an AI thing." You're selling the part everyone
underestimates: **making the agent reliable enough to trust** — evals,
guardrails, observability, cost control, data handling. That's worth a premium,
and your finance background is the proof you can do it.
