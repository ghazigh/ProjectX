# The wedge offer — Instant Lead Response & Booking Agent

One specific, named, ROI-quantified offer. Not "AI consulting." Specificity is
what makes a cold buyer say yes.

## The offer, in one sentence

> "I install an AI agent that replies to every new lead in under 60 seconds,
> qualifies them, and books the job straight onto your calendar — so you stop
> losing customers to whoever answers first."

Working product name options: **FirstReply**, **LeadCatch**, **NeverMissed**.

## Who it's for

Local service businesses that (a) get inbound leads via web form, chat, or
missed calls, and (b) lose them to slow follow-up. Strong niches:

- Home services: cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pest control, landscaping
- Health/appearance: dental, med spas, physio, chiropractic
- Professional: law firms (PI, family, immigration), accountants
- Trades & B2B local: solar, security installers, commercial cleaning

## The ROI math (let the prospect plug in their own numbers)

Don't quote stats you can't back — make the buyer do the arithmetic on *their*
business. It's more honest and more convincing:

```
Leads per month that don't get an instant reply:   ___  (e.g. 40)
% you currently lose to slow/no follow-up:          ___  (e.g. 30% = 12)
Average value of one closed job:                    $___ (e.g. $400)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Revenue leaking out the bottom each month:          $___ (e.g. $4,800)
```

If the agent recovers even a third of that, it pays for itself many times over.
That's the whole pitch: **you're not selling AI, you're selling recovered
revenue that's already leaking.** (Speed-to-lead matters — "first responder wins
most of the deal" is well documented in sales research; cite a real source if you
put a specific figure in writing, don't invent one.)

## Packages (micro-offer stack)

| Tier | What's included | Price guide |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Setup / build** | Configure the agent to their business, connect their lead source + calendar, test, go live | **$1,500 – $3,500** one-time |
| **Pilot** | 2-week paid pilot on a subset of leads, with a results readout — lowers the buyer's risk and gets you in the door | **$500 – $1,000** |
| **Managed retainer** | Hosting, monitoring, monthly tuning, conversation review, support | **$300 – $900 / month** |

Stack: pilot → setup → retainer. The retainer is the compounding asset and the
reason this isn't just trading hours for money.

## Path to $9,000 (concrete)

You only need a handful of yeses:

- **3 setups** at $2,000 avg = **$6,000** + **5 retainers** at $600/mo = **$3,000/mo recurring** → you clear $9k inside the first cycle and keep ~$3k/mo after.
- Or **2 setups** ($4k) + **1 done-for-you build** for a multi-location client ($5k) = **$9k** from three conversations.

The $330 "first dollar" is just a pilot deposit on call #1 — proof the mechanism
works, then scale the same motion.

## Why this becomes the product

Every client you onboard is one more `configs/<client>.json`. After 2-3 bespoke
installs you'll know exactly what's identical across them — at which point the
config + onboarding becomes a self-serve product (client fills a form → agent
provisions). Services funded it; the product compounds it. Same artifact.
